## The problem

The ceiling face is a library maze: to reach the sword you walk on bookcase tops, and three of the cases roll between two stops when someone turns a winch on the floor. A case on top is a bridge; on the floor it is a wall. The mechanism had to carry players standing on it, refuse to crush anyone, stop when someone steps into its lane, and look identical on host and client.

## What the player sees

<Figure id="library-lane" caption="Rolling case K1 on its rail. The quill marker on its face matches the marker on its winch." />

- Hold E on a winch: the case rolls, slowly out of a stop and slowly into the next.
- If anyone (player or guard) is in the lane, the winch refuses and the player says why.
- Step in front of a moving case and it stops in front of you, then continues when you leave.
- Stand on top and you ride along.

Try the puzzle it creates on the <a href={u("/levels/library-maze/")}>library maze page</a>.

## Why this design

- **State, not motion, is replicated.** The `Carriage` component is not driven by movement replication; each end positions it from the state and the synchronised server time. Bandwidth is a handful of packets per turn and both ends agree exactly.
- **The rejection box is 6 cm smaller** than the case on each side and 6 cm lower on top, so a player standing on the case or leaning against its side does not block it.
- **Riders are exempt from blocking.** A vertical case (the lift) sweeps exactly where its passengers stand; a pawn whose movement base is this case is not an obstacle.
- **Solvable by construction.** The layout lives in a pure-Python file with its own solver and 97 checks: the minimum winch order, dead-end states, floor regions for every shelf configuration, sprint-gap rules (a sprinting body can clear 2 m, not 4 m).

## Limits and next steps

- A remote client turning a winch was injected through scripts, not real input; latency and packet loss while riding were not measured.
- The physical hold-E check on the shipped map did not register progress in one injected probe and was not counted as passed.
