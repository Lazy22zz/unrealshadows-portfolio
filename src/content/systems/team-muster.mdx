## The problem

Originally, completing the third Stage 1 task travelled the whole session to Stage 2 immediately — even if your partner was in the middle of a side quest. The fix needed: "doors open, then wait until every player is in the zone for 3 seconds". Stage 2 needs the same thing twice: everyone has *left* the spawn room (to start the round) and everyone is *back* (to finish).

## What the player sees

- A ring with one segment per living player; your own segment is always at 12 o'clock.
- When everyone is in, the ring closes and burns down; leave and it breaks with a shake.

## Why this design

- **One component, two modes** (`AllPresent`, `AllAbsent`). The arm condition differs per use; the waiting logic does not.
- **A cheap poll covers what overlaps cannot.** A player can die or disconnect *inside* the zone; neither fires an overlap event, but both change the denominator. A 0.25 s recount only runs while someone is inside or the muster is armed, instead of hooking five death-state transitions.
- **Re-validate at commit.** The 3 s timer always fires once scheduled; without a final check, a team that had already split would be travelled anyway.
- **No silent soft-lock.** If a map is missing its gate zone, the GameMode logs an error and falls back to the old immediate travel — a missing actor must not leave a session stuck at 3/3 forever.

<Callout kind="info">
The state is replicated as one struct (armed, present, required, commit end time, sequence) so the HUD never sees half an update.
</Callout>
