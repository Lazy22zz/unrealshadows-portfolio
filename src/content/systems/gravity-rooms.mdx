## The problem

The level is built on all six inner faces of a cube. Players walk up a wall, across the ceiling, and fall "down" into a different room. That needs: per-room gravity for characters, smooth transitions at the edges, AI that can path-find on a wall, abilities that only affect the room you are in, and a way to recover anyone who ends up somewhere with the wrong gravity.

## What the player sees

<Figure id="library-pad" caption="The ceiling face of the cube, seen from its landing pad. The floor room is 48 m above the player's head." />

- Walk off a 35 cm golden step on a tower and you tip over onto the north wall.
- Get flung by a drain lane across the cube and land on the opposite wall.
- Guards patrol walls and the ceiling like any other floor.

## Key classes and functions

| Piece | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| `AUS_GravityZone` | Oriented room box; its up axis defines the room's gravity |
| `AUS_GravitySeam` | Curved transition along one cube edge |
| `UUS_GravitySubsystem` | `EvaluateSeams`, `ResolveStrandedGravity`, `HasSeams` |
| `UUS_CharacterMovementComponent::UpdateRoomGravity` | Applies the result with `SetGravityDirection` |
| Nav twin | Every collision piece on a wall gets a copy laid flat far below the level for nav-mesh baking |

## Why this design

- **Zero regression by construction.** The default-gravity branch is the original engine path; the new logic only runs when a map has seams.
- **Recover, don't special-case.** After any teleport (portals, rescue, debug) the stranded resolver puts the correct gravity back, instead of every feature remembering to set it.
- **Geometry is generated, then audited.** `BuildStage2GravityCube.py` builds the cube and its twin; `AnalyseStage2Clipping.py` checks oriented-box overlaps (2 cm tolerance) and the clearance of the five gravity lanes. A whitelist documents every intended overlap.

<Callout kind="warn" title="A real bug this exposed">
<code>FAIMoveRequest::SetGoalActor</code> is a no-op on an already initialised request. On walls, guards "saw but didn't chase" until <code>MoveTo</code> built a fresh request for actor goals.
</Callout>

## Limits and next steps

- Edges are a classic seam: two faces' 9 m bands overlap, so lip points must leave the neighbouring band.
- The cube is built by a generator plus many narrow patch scripts; a full regeneration is not identical to the shipped map, so changes are made as new guarded patches (each checks the map's file hash before writing).
