## What the player does

The player arrives in the **past** cistern, at the bottom of a well. The goal — a relic sealed in a crypt under the central pier — is visible from the first minute through an oculus in the well floor, but the only way down is a lift that opens when **three time moments** (one per era) have been collected. The relic's seal is held by **four fragments** hidden in dead ends across the eras.

## Design rules

- **Each era is a different maze.** Places in different eras may share coordinates; that never connects them.
- **Exactly three junctions, each joining two eras** (A: past↔present, B: present↔future, C: past↔future). They sit on different heights (+6.6 m, −1.4 m, +13.4 m), so the three eras are threaded vertically.
- **No floor slabs.** Height is continuous from the crypt (−7 m) to the crown (+15.6 m). The old "three storeys" survive only as a height budget.
- **The Well of Hours is the spine of spatial memory.** Three beats: from the past well bottom you look up at the sanctum door 15 m overhead; in the future you see the same well again from a broken span; from the Heart you look down at where you started.
- **One-way drops and doors create Dark Souls-style shortcuts.** A crypt door can only be pushed open from the inside; once open it is two-way.
- **No soft-locks.** The solver explores every reachable state (positions × eras × keys) and proves that the goal is reachable from all of them.

## How it is built in Unreal

<Figure id="temple-eras" caption="The same spots in the past, present and future art passes." />

The three eras are **three side-by-side copies** of the temple. A junction is an `AUS_TrialPortal` with `bPreserveRelativePlacement`: it shifts the player by the offset between copies, keeping position, facing, camera and velocity — the same predicted, black-screen-free traversal used everywhere else in the game.

Seeing into another era uses two techniques, picked per window by a rule:

- **Wall + echo** when the watched spot is solid and unreachable in the viewer's era: a single-sided wall with a sealed cavity behind it, and `AUS_TempleTimeEcho` mirrors the other player's pose into it. Almost free to render.
- **SceneCapture windows** everywhere else (`AUS_TempleTimeWindow`), placed beside the path rather than on it, and only capturing while on screen.

<Callout kind="info" title="Iteration history">
V7 used three stacked floors and a three-era junction; V8 made every junction strictly pairwise; V9.1 threw out floors entirely for a route-first 3-D maze after the first playtest felt like "three slabs with stairs"; V9.2 was validated in a two-process technical slice; V9.4 added the time moments and fragments after players said "I can't tell what I'm meant to do"; V9.5 moved the relic into the crypt with an intro shot and a lift-only entrance.
</Callout>

## Limitations

- The plan view flattens height; colour shows height, and hovering a path shows both ends' heights.
- Window and echo placement, lighting and the intro camera are only described here; they need to be seen in engine.
